# Prompts

Copy them in order. Replace the parts in [brackets]. Each prompt is one step; check the result before you type the next one. The steps match [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md).

## 1. Plan

I want to make a 30-second promo video for my brand, [your brand]. My website is [your website], and my logo is in this folder. At the end, people should feel [one feeling]. Write a plan first: the scenes, what the voice says in each one, and how long each one is. Don't make anything yet.

## 2. Voice

Make the voice-over with ElevenLabs, using the voice Eric. Make one audio file for each sentence, in the best quality.

Free option:
Make the voice-over with Kokoro instead. It's free and runs on my computer. Install whatever you need. One audio file for each sentence.

## 3. Timeline

No prompt. The tempo (`bpm`) and each scene's minimum length (`min`) are in `script.json`, and the lengths come from the voice files from step 2.

## 4. Music

Make 30 seconds of music with ElevenLabs. Calm and cinematic, with a strong beat every 4 seconds.

Free option:
Use the song in my folder as the music. Cut it to 30 seconds and fade out the end.

## 5. Picture

The animation:
Draw the animations yourself, using the shapes from my logo and my brand colours. No templates.

For titles, captions and screen scenes: use HyperFrames, and restyle any block to my brand.

The video clips:
Find free video clips on Pixabay that fit my scenes. Show me a grid of options with numbers, so I can pick.

## 6. Mix

Put it all together into one video. Change scenes on the beat, turn the music down when the voice talks, and make it loud enough for YouTube. Save it as an MP4.

## 7. Notes and the final render

Write one note at a time, in normal words. For example:
- Make scene 3 shorter, and use simpler words.
- Redo sentence 4, a bit slower.
- The logo comes in too late. Start it on the first beat.

When nothing bugs you any more, ask for the final render:
I'm happy with it. Render the whole video in full quality, in [1080p or 4K], and make the final mix. Save it as one MP4.
